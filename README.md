# XenPips

Living pets for your desktop.

XenPips puts a small 3D pet on your screen. It walks along the bottom above the taskbar or Dock, naps when you are busy, answers your clicks and keeps quiet while you watch a film or join a call. Windows and Mac, free.

**Site and downloads: [xenpips.com](https://xenpips.com)**

## Download

Every release is in [Releases](../../releases), with the same files as on [xenpips.com/download](https://xenpips.com/download):

| System | File | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| macOS 12 or newer | `XenPips-<version>-macOS.dmg` | Intel and Apple silicon. Signed and notarized by Apple. Open the .dmg and drag XenPips into Applications: from there it updates itself. |
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `XenPips-<version>-Windows.zip` | Unpack and run `XenPips.exe`. Not in the Microsoft Store yet, so SmartScreen may warn on the first start: More info, then Run anyway. |

No account is needed to start.

## The pets

- **PIP Starter**: a curious little robot gull. Free, no account needed.
- **NOX**: a glossy black robot cat with glowing cyan eyes. Testers first.
- **Skydex PIP**: your PIP from the [Skydex](https://skydex.online) flight game, with the colours, gear and stage you earned there. Free with a Skydex account.

More on [xenpips.com/pets](https://xenpips.com/pets).
